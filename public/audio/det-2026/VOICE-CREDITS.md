# Audio credits / Créditos de audio

The original English scripts for Verbo NEXT TOEFL practice forms 2 and 3 were
written for this platform. Audio was synthesized locally with Piper; no actor
endorses Verbo NEXT or these practice tests.

- Piper engine, version 2023.11.14-2, by Michael Hansen and contributors:
  https://github.com/rhasspy/piper (MIT license).
- U.S. English voices: Piper LibriTTS-R medium, trained on the LibriTTS-R corpus.
  Dataset by Yuma Koizumi, Heiga Zen, Shigeki Karita, Yifan Ding, Kohei Yatabe,
  Nobuyuki Morioka, Michiel Bacchiani, Yu Zhang, Wei Han, and Ankur Bapna,
  "LibriTTS-R: A Restored Multi-Speaker Text-to-Speech Corpus" (2023); CC BY 4.0.
  https://www.openslr.org/141/
  https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/libritts_r/medium
- British English voices: Piper VCTK medium, based on the CSTR VCTK corpus,
  by Junichi Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux, and Kirsten MacDonald; CC BY 4.0.
  https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443
  https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/vctk/medium
- License for the credited datasets: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The recordings are newly synthesized renditions of original scripts, with
speaker selection, timing, and dialogue assembly by Verbo NEXT. The dataset
authors and original speakers did not record these scripts. Per-file voice,
duration, and checksum information is in `voices-forms-2-3.json`.
